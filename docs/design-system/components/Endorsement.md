# Endorsement

Endorsement is the "An Upspire product" line that ties every product back to the studio.

- Place it once per product, in the site footer and on the sign-in screen, under the product's own lockup.
- It is always small (`caption` size, `ink-soft`) and always below the product name, never beside or above it. The product leads; Upspire endorses.
- **Provide:** optional `href` (defaults to https://upspire.studio).
- Upspire Studio's own pages don't use it.
