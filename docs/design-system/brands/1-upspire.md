# Upspire Studio

**Role:** the parent brand. A strategy and technology studio that builds and endorses the product family.
**Speaks to:** nonprofit and civic leaders, funders and partners.
**Personality:** confident, strategic, unfussy.

## Color

| Role | Token | Light |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | `upspire-primary` | Ink `#221E19` |
| Hover | `upspire-primary-hover` | `#000000` |
| Tint | `upspire-tint` | `#ECE8F6` |
| Accent | `upspire-accent` | Iris `#6A58A6` |
| On primary | `upspire-on-primary` | `#FAF6EE` |

Ratio: 75 paper, 20 ink, 5 iris. Iris is a nod to the purple in the original Upspire watercolor mark; it only ever appears as the spark.

## Mark and type

The upward chevron with an iris spark. Wordmark set in Fraunces 600. The earlier Syne wordmark and the watercolor mark are retired from product-facing use and kept for reference in `brand/logos-current/`.

## Surfaces

upspire.studio, proposals, decks, the family overview, and the endorsement line inside each product.

## Do and don't

- Do present the products as a family, each in its own color.
- Don't put the Upspire mark larger than a product's mark on a product surface.
