# Board Manager

**Role:** nonprofit board management: roster, candidate pipeline, committees and compliance.
**Speaks to:** board chairs, executive directors and board secretaries.
**Personality:** steady, organized, trustworthy.

## Color

| Role | Token | Light |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | `nbm-primary` | Navy `#2B3A5C` |
| Hover | `nbm-primary-hover` | `#1A2540` |
| Tint | `nbm-tint` | Mist `#EEF1F8` |
| Accent | `nbm-accent` | Terracotta `#C4603A` |
| On primary | `nbm-on-primary` | `#FFFFFF` |

Ratio: 70 paper, 20 ink, 8 navy, 2 terracotta.

## Mark

A round table with six seats; the head seat in terracotta. This replaces the shipped icon, whose teal and grey (`#004754`, `#b4b4b4`) match none of the palette.

## Voice

Calm and procedural without jargon. Explain governance terms the first time ("quorum: the minimum number of members who must be present").
