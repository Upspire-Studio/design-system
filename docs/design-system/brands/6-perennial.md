# Perennial Giving

**Role:** a donor retention engine. Currently a founding-member waitlist.
**Speaks to:** development directors and small-shop executive directors who raise money.
**Personality:** warm, patient, nurturing.

## Color

| Role | Token | Light |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | `perennial-primary` | Berry `#5E3350` |
| Hover | `perennial-primary-hover` | `#47253C` |
| Tint | `perennial-tint` | `#F3E8EF` |
| Accent | `perennial-accent` | Marigold `#D49A2A` |
| On primary | `perennial-on-primary` | `#FFFFFF` |

Ratio: 70 paper, 18 ink, 9 berry, 3 marigold. Berry replaces the current evergreen (`#2E5E4E`), which sat too close to Volunteer Shift Manager's forest; perennial flowers in berry and gold keep the growing-season idea.

## Mark

A two-leaf sprout over a ground line; the upper leaf in marigold. It replaces the gradient raster logo with a flat vector on the shared grid.

## Voice

Talks about donors as people and relationships over years, never as revenue to extract. "Keep donors giving, year after year."
